Workout

Support

Workout is a log for the sets you lift. This page is the support address for the iPhone app.

Log a set

Swipe the weight, the reps, and the effort. Tap Log set. Undo removes the set you just logged. Finish writes the time and starts another session. Finish stays hidden until the session has a set.

The line, History, and Progress

The line is one plan, from this week’s load to a target. History keeps finished days. Progress charts the weight and the reps of each finished lift. An open session is not on the chart until you finish it.

Pounds and kilograms

Tap lb / kg on the card. The numbers are stored in pounds and shown in the unit you pick.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in from Settings, or from Keep this log after a new subscription. The lifts, the workouts, the plan, the goal, and the cost then live in your private iCloud database as well as on the phone. A second phone with the same Apple ID can pick that log up after you sign in there.

Subscription

Plan workout and a custom lift name use the subscription. Catalog lifts, logging, finishing, and history do not. The annual plan includes 14 days free. The weekly price is shown on the paywall. Apple charges the subscription. Cancel it in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Restore purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. Deleting the log does not cancel the subscription.

Delete the log

After you are signed in, Settings offers Delete the log. It asks first. Confirming clears the lifts, the workouts, and the plan on that phone and in iCloud, then signs out.
