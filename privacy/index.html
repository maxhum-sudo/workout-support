Workout

Privacy

Updated November 10, 2026. Workout is an iPhone app from the developer of com.maximillianhum.WorkoutLogger. This page describes what the app stores and where it goes.

On the phone

The lifts, the sets, the workouts, and the growth plan are stored on the phone. The goal, the cost, the weight unit, the colors, and whether the opening guide is finished are stored on the phone as well. After Sign in with Apple, the Apple ID’s user identifier and the name Apple provides are stored on the phone.

iCloud

Sign in is optional until you choose it. After you sign in, the lifts, the workouts, the sets, the growth plan, the goal, and the cost are copied to your private iCloud database, container iCloud.com.maximillianhum.WorkoutLogger, in the zone WorkoutLog. That database belongs to the Apple ID you used. Workout does not put the log in a public database and does not run a server of its own.

Delete the log clears those records on the phone and in that private database, then signs out. The App Store subscription stays until you cancel it in App Store settings.

Speech

Dictate, on a custom lift name and on the cost field, uses the on-device speech recognizer. The audio is not sent to a Workout server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether the annual or weekly plan is active. It does not receive your card number. The annual plan includes 14 days free. The price is the one shown on the paywall.

What leaves the phone

The log leaves the phone when you sign in, and then only to your private iCloud database. The app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

Children

Workout is a training log. It is not directed at children under 13.
